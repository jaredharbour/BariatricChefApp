# Product Context

## Why This Exists
Bariatric surgery patients are given complex dietary guidelines but have no easy way to find recipes that comply. General recipe apps don't understand stage-based progression, dumping syndrome risk, or protein-first eating rules. This app fills that gap.

## Post-Bariatric Dietary Progression
| Stage | Timing | What's Allowed |
|-------|--------|----------------|
| PreOp | Pre-surgery | Low-calorie prep diet |
| Stage 1: Clear Liquid | Days 1–2 post-op | Broth, water, diluted juice |
| Stage 2: Full Liquid | Days 3–14 | Protein shakes, thinned yogurt |
| Stage 3: Pureed | Weeks 2–4 | Blended foods, 4oz max |
| Stage 4: Soft Food | Weeks 4–8 | Soft cooked foods, 4–6oz |
| Stage 5: Regular | 8+ weeks | Normal texture, small portions |

## Key Dietary Rules
- **Protein first**: Every meal should prioritize protein (60–80g/day goal)
- **No dumping syndrome foods**: High sugar triggers nausea/dumping for bypass patients
- **Small portions**: Stomach pouch holds 2–4oz post-op, expanding over time
- **No carbonation**: Stretches stomach pouch
- **Slow eating**: No gulping or drinking with meals

## Surgery Type Differences
- **Gastric Bypass (RNY)**: Highest dumping syndrome risk, requires strictest sugar limits
- **Sleeve Gastrectomy**: Moderate restrictions, similar progression
- **Adjustable Band**: Slowest restriction, most adjustable
- **Duodenal Switch**: Most aggressive malabsorption, highest protein requirements

## User Experience Goals
- Patient enters surgery type and current stage on first login
- App only shows appropriate recipes — no filtering required by user
- Nutritional warnings surfaced as informational, not blocking
- Simple, medical-tone UI (not foodie/lifestyle aesthetic)
